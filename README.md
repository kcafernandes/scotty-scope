# scotty-scope
Building a UCR-specific course intelligence website that combines course info, professor info, historical offerings, prerequisites, ratings, and student feedback into one organized platform

---

Planning a class schedule at UCR is often very time consuming. Students often switch between the course catalog, Degree Audit, Reddit, Rate My Professors, and other websites to research courses and instructors. They may also need to determine which quarters a course is typically offered, compare professor ratings, understand prerequisites, and estimate course difficulty. Overall, course registration is a very tedious process that shouldn't be this difficult :')

By building Scotty Scope, I hope to simplify this process for fellow UCR students by making all relevant course information accessible in one place.

> This project is currently in development, and its features and scope may change.
